# 🍽️ Restaurant Sales Analysis Using PostgreSQL

## 📖 Overview

This project demonstrates the use of **PostgreSQL** to analyze restaurant sales data and answer real-world business questions. Through SQL queries, I explored menu performance, customer ordering behavior, pricing trends, and revenue insights to support data-driven decision-making.

The project showcases core SQL skills, including data exploration, filtering, aggregations, joins, subqueries, and business reporting.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze restaurant sales data using PostgreSQL.
- Explore menu pricing and category distribution.
- Identify the most and least popular menu items.
- Analyze customer ordering patterns.
- Discover high-value orders and purchasing behavior.
- Generate business insights through SQL queries.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The analysis is based on two relational tables:

### **menu_items**
Contains information about each menu item, including:
- Menu Item ID
- Item Name
- Category
- Price

### **order_details**
Contains transactional order data, including:
- Order ID
- Order Date
- Order Time
- Item ID

## 📊 Business Questions Answered

This project answers the following business questions:

- How many items are available on the menu?
- What are the most and least expensive menu items?
- How many Italian dishes are available?
- What are the cheapest and most expensive Italian dishes?
- How many dishes are available in each category?
- What is the average price of dishes by category?
- What is the order date range?
- How many orders and items were recorded during the analysis period?
- Which orders contained the highest number of items?
- How many orders contained more than 12 items?
- Which menu items were ordered the most and least?
- Which orders generated the highest revenue?
- What items were included in the highest-spending orders?

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- Menu pricing varies significantly across categories, with premium dishes contributing more to overall revenue.
- Certain menu items consistently outperform others, making them strong candidates for promotional campaigns.
- High-value orders contain multiple premium-priced menu items, indicating opportunities for upselling.
- Customer purchasing patterns provide valuable insights for menu optimization and pricing strategies.
- SQL can effectively transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve restaurant performance:

- Promote high-performing menu items through marketing campaigns and combo offers to maximize revenue.
- Review the pricing and popularity of low-performing dishes to determine whether they should be repriced, improved, or removed from the menu.
- Introduce upselling strategies by pairing popular items with complementary products to increase average order value.
- Monitor customer ordering patterns regularly to identify changing preferences and seasonal trends.
- Continue leveraging SQL-driven reporting to support informed business decisions and optimize restaurant operations.
